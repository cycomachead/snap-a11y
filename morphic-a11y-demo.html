Morphic Accessibility (AT) Demo — Phase 1 core

Everything inside the bordered canvas is drawn by Morphic. A parallel, invisible DOM tree (see #morphic_a11y_root_* in the inspector) mirrors the accessible morphs for the screen reader.

Try this

  • Tab / Shift+Tab — move between widgets. A 2px blue focus ring appears (keyboard only).
  • Click a button with the mouse — no ring (focus-visible semantics).
  • Enter / Space — activate the focused button.
  • Tab to Editable field, press Enter to edit, type, then Esc/Enter. The ring tracks the field while editing.
  • Tab to the Combobox, use ↑/↓ — this drives aria-activedescendant.
  • Tab to Menu ▾, Enter to open, then ↑/↓ to move (announced), → into the submenu, Esc to close. Or right-click anywhere for the dev menu.
  • Turn on VoiceOver (⌘F5) or NVDA and explore.
